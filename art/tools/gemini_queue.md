## What made frames flicker, and what fixed it (read before cleaning any sheet)

- **Gemini repaints every frame.** Dark armour shimmers and a head's outline pops, even with every box lined up
  (Elvis's first side run: 1000-1400 pixels changed per frame pair above the hips, the eye 5 px sideways). Where the
  upper body barely moves (runs, walks, idles), build the sheet with `stride.py` from parts that are never repainted:
  one head and one upper body moved by the bob, only the legs from Gemini's frames (`art/elvis/stride_run_S.json`:
  100-280 pixels change per pair, all of them moves). For attacks, where the whole body moves, Gemini's frames stay,
  but reuse identical guide poses (`--pick`, guard at both ends) so those frames are pixel-identical.
- **One colour hugging every outline** (`#221616`, 10% of the old side run) is a clean-up left-over, not a design:
  check every new sheet for it; `stride.py`'s `"fringe"` removes it.
- **Feet off the floor:** `--anchor head` lets Gemini's leg lengths set the feet. Use `--anchor body` for attacks; in
  `stride.py` drop legs onto the feet row (`"feet": true`).
- **Movement that reads at game size** comes from the bob, a 1 px sway, the weapon a frame behind the body, the knee and
  boot moving apart, and the cape and hair swinging a frame behind. Check every sheet at 1x and 3x, not only 4x.
- **The guide decides the size:** a figure Gemini paints under about 190 px tall loses its face at 80 px. Don't pack
  more rows to save a picture.
- **Kneels come back as tall as a stand** (human Vulcan's kneel, 25 Oct): scaled to its height the man shrank to a
  narrow child; scaled to his head and shoulders he knelt 78 px tall. Put the master standing in front of the
  mannequins at the guide's scale (`art/tools/guide_with_ref.py`), ask for "the standing man's head and shoulders,
  kneeling makes him shorter", build with `--scale-frame 0 --height <the master's>` and cut the standing cell off
  (`art/vulcan_human/notes.md`). Do the same for the boss's kneel (B07) and statue (B06), so the boss and the man under
  his armour kneel at the same size in C3.
- **A kneel is only low when it is hunched** (human Vulcan's kneel redo, 25 Oct evening): from this camera an upright
  kneel is nearly as tall as a stand (the front thigh points at the camera and runs down the screen), and Gemini draws

## Clean-up recipes

`P="uv run --with pillow --with numpy --with scipy python"`, run from the project folder. `<c>` is `elvis`,
`vulcan_boss` or `vulcan_human`; `<H>` is `--height 80` for Elvis, `--height 84 --scale-frame 0` for the boss (the guard
frame then stands as tall as his master, 84 px from the crown's rim to his feet: compare the body heights the run
prints), `--height 80 --quant octree` for human Vulcan. A kneel's guide has the master standing first
(`guide_with_ref.py`): build it with `--scale-frame 0` and the master's height (84 for the boss, 80 for human Vulcan),
then cut the standing cell off; the knelt body should come out about 0.75 of the standing one. Cells: Elvis 240x160, the boss 288x192, human Vulcan 128x96 (the manifests keep them).

- **R1, one view** (one row, or the rows of one view read left to right, top row first):
  `$P art/sprites.py art/<c>/raw/<raw>.jpeg --cols <figures per row> --rows <rows> --split shapes --anchor body <H>
  --palette art/<c>/palette.png --out art/<c>/<key>.png --manifest art/<c>/manifest.json --key <key> --fps <fps>
  --no-loop` (or `--loop`; `--pick` drops a front-on copy of the master or a repeated pad figure).
- **R2, front row over back row:** cut each row with `$P art/tools/band.py art/<c>/raw/<raw>.jpeg
  art/_work/<raw>_<F|B>.png <y0> <y1>` (the row's top and bottom in the picture), then R1 on each band with `--rows 1`.
- **R3, front, side and back rows:** as R2 with three bands.
- Then, every sheet: the eyes (front frames: `art/tools/eyes.py <sheet> --cell W H --master art/<c>/master.png
  --master-touchup art/<c>/touchup.json --head ... --frames ... --out art/<c>/touchup_<key>.json`; side frames:
  `eyes.py ... --side`), rebuilt with `--touchup`; the holds and hit frames written into `manifest.json` by hand
  (`sprites.py` keeps holds that are already there); a front attack whose weapon dips below his feet keeps those rows:
  build it with the feet N rows higher and add `"below": N` to its key (`docs/PROGRESS.md`, Task 6); the review gate at
  1x, 3x and 4x next to the idle (same character, both eyes, details, motion, no fringe, feet on the line); a GIF with
  `art/tools/gif.py` into `docs/progress/`; the notes in `art/<c>/notes.md`; commit with a `docs/PROGRESS.md` entry.
